# Cyclops enemies

Original RuneSpeak cyclops artwork generated using the built-in image-generation tool, October 1, 2026. Three models (Clubber, Rockthrower, Ironhide), each in teal, moss, violet and ember. Six poses: idle, step, wind-up, attack, hurt and defeated. Source sheets are 1536x1024 RGBA PNGs.

Style reference: https://blog.duolingo.com/shape-language-duolingos-art-style/ — rounded simplified shapes and exaggerated readable features; no brand characters copied. Based on user-approved cyclops concepts.

frames.js records measured full silhouette rectangles and foot anchors. Some poses extend outside nominal cells; do not replace these rectangles with an equal grid crop. Palette selection is seeded separately from model selection. No stats differ by palette. Asset loading is lazy, with same-model teal fallback for a missing recolor.
