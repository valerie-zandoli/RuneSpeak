# RuneSpeak Knight artwork

The approved October 1 knight replaces the earlier knight sheet. He retains his brown hair, raised silver helmet, silver-and-blue armor, sword and blue shield, with larger white eyes matching the Ranger and Wizard.

`knight-approved.png` preserves the approved concept. `knight-poses.png` is the generated 1536 x 1024 transparent animation sheet. The built-in image-generation tool produced six poses: idle, walk, anticipation, strike, defend and salute.

See `knight-art.json` for the full production prompt, measured source rectangles, feet anchors, timing, frame reuse and reduced-motion behavior. Strike and salute use measured crops into empty padding to preserve complete swords without adjacent figures.

The existing Knight ID, 120 HP, 6 block, equipment, saves and audio remain in use. Selection, HUD and home illustration use the first sheet cell. The renderer uses all six poses.
